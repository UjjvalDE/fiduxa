| **Q1** | **Which legal model for cleaners?** Fiduxa sets the price, the time window and the commission — strong indicators of dependent employment under German law; the EU Platform Work Directive must be transposed by 2 Dec 2026. | Wrong answer = back-payment of social contributions, fines, invoices invalid.

# Competitor & Technology Benchmark

*Research date: 21 November 2026. Only public pages were read. Confidence: **High** = read on the company's own site today; **Medium** = independent review/test source or older; **Low** = single anecdotal review or general knowledge. Nothing below claims knowledge of internal systems.*

## 1. Helpling (helpling.de) — Germany, marketplace of self-employed cleaners

| Topic | What the public site says | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Booking flow | Postcode → date, duration, frequency → compare profiles & prices → send a *booking request* to one cleaner (not an instant booking). Bookable ~24 h in advance. | High |
| Cleaner choice | Customers compare profiles (photo, reviews, price); no name search; profile links can be shared. | High |
| Pricing | Cleaners set their own hourly rate (Berlin average shown: 22,00 €/h; range 16,90–35 €/h) + Helpling service fee 2,99 € per booking. 50 m² ≈ 2 h ≈ 34,90 €. | High |
| Cancellation | Free > 48 h; 24–48 h up to 25 %; 12–24 h up to 50 %; < 12 h up to 100 %. | High |
| Trust | Liability insurance up to 5 M € per booking (damage < 500 € settled with the cleaner directly); "verification process" for cleaners (unspecified); invoices auto-generated for § 35a; Trustpilot 4.5–4.6; "10,000+ cleaners, 200+ cities"; live chat Mon–Sat. | High |
| Legal model | Cleaners are self-employed; Helpling is "a marketplace". Criticised for years (Stiftung Warentest 2018, Böckler study, netzpolitik/ETUC 2022) for possible *Scheinselbstständigkeit*; a court classified Helpling cleaners as temp workers in one case. | Medium |
| Reliability complaints | Recurring Trustpilot themes: no-shows, unmotivated cleaners, disputed charges. | Medium |

**What Fiduxa can learn:** (a) the § 35a invoice is table stakes in Germany — Helpling generates it automatically; Fiduxa does not; (b) tiered late-cancellation fees protect cleaners' income; (c) the "booking request to a specific cleaner" model creates waiting and rejections — Fiduxa's instant-confirm model is a real advantage *if* supply is guaranteed; (d) the self-employed model is legally exposed — Fiduxa must decide its model deliberately.

Sources: [helpling.de](https://www.helpling.de/), [helpling.de/preise](https://www.helpling.de/preise), [helpling.de/faq](https://www.helpling.de/faq), [test.de Putz-Portale (2018)](https://www.test.de/Putz-Portale-im-Test-4767070-0/), [Trustpilot Helpling](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/helpling.de), [netzpolitik.org 2022](https://netzpolitik.org/2022/plattformarbeit-wie-neue-eu-regeln-helpling-treffen-koennten/), [Böckler Impuls](https://www.boeckler.de/de/boeckler-impuls-kaum-schutz-fur-reinigungskrafte-45297.htm).

## 2. Batmaid (batmaid.ch) — Switzerland, employer model

| Topic | What the public site says | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Booking flow | Duration, frequency, date/time → Batmaid matches "the best available cleaner" → instant booking; move-out via form with extras, no quote. | High |
| Recurring | Weekly/bi-weekly: "the same Batmaid every time"; on illness/holiday the customer can skip or get another cleaner. | High |
| Cancellation (consumer) | Free > 28 days; CHF 20 between 28 and 8 days; full price ≤ 7 days. (Much stricter than Helpling.) | High |
| Employment | Since 2021 Batmaid is the legal employer: permanent contracts under the sector CBA, sickness/accident cover, damage insurance, guaranteed paid hours even if a client cancels. Publishes a cost breakdown (76 % salary/social contributions, 9 % VAT & fees, 7 % support, 6 % tech, 1 % marketing, 1 % profit, Geneva figures). | High |
| Trust | "4.6/5, 293 029 verified reviews since 2015"; customer checklist to customise the service; app to manage bookings "without calls or emails". | High |
| Reliability complaints | Trustpilot CH ≈ 3.5/5 (~2 000 reviews): no-shows, late arrivals, cancellations by Batmaid; employee reviews criticise pay/late payments. | Medium |

**What Fiduxa can learn:** (a) the published cost breakdown is a powerful trust device — Fiduxa already has the data (finance page) and could publish its own honest split; (b) "same cleaner every time" is the retention engine for recurring customers — Fiduxa stores a preference but does not enforce it; (c) a strict cancellation policy with a long free window is accepted by customers when framed as protecting the cleaner; (d) even an employer model does not fix no-shows — operations (check-in, replacement automation) do.

Sources: [batmaid.ch](https://batmaid.ch/en/), [about-us](https://batmaid.ch/en/about-us), [cancellation-conditions](https://batmaid.ch/en/cancellation-conditions), [same cleaner (B2B help)](https://batmaid.ch/en/help-section/will-the-same-batmaid-come-every-time-b2b), [Trustpilot Batmaid](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/batmaid.ch), [Indeed Batmaid reviews](https://ch.indeed.com/cmp/Batmaid/reviews).

## 3. Zenjob (zenjob.com) — Germany, student temp staffing (workforce ideas)

| Topic | What the public site says | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | App only: email + mobile confirmation, skill checks (e.g. language test), documents (enrolment, ID), identity verification via a partner (Identity Trust, TAN signature), IBAN/tax ID/social security before first shift. Free online trainings unlock job types. | High |
| Matching | Workers apply in-app; booking is binding; returning workers who did the same job/location get **instant priority** unless rated poorly; shifts are held for returning workers first; otherwise "AI-supported" best-fit; one shift per day. | High |
| Reliability rules | Check-in/check-out is the basis of pay; cancel < 48 h or > 30 % of shifts → deactivation; 3 strikes → removal; sick note required same day; no-show after start → immediate deactivation. Claims only 1 % no-shows. | High |
| Payout | 50 % of gross wage 2–3 business days after the shift, rest by the 15th of the next month. | High |
| Company side | Free cancellation > 48 h, otherwise charged; staffing forecast "ML over 60 criteria, up to 95 % accuracy"; talent pool of workers from the last 120 days. | High |
| Legal | Temp work (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung): Zenjob is the employer, 70-day short-term rule, 6-month "revolving door" rule. | High |
| Worker sentiment | Mixed: praised for fast pay and flexibility; criticised for support and cancellations (Indeed 3.5/5; small Trustpilot samples). | Medium |

**What Fiduxa can learn (directly transferable):** (a) *returning-worker priority* → "preferred cleaner first, then open board"; (b) *check-in/check-out as the basis of pay* → honest actual durations, no-show detection within minutes; (c) explicit, published reliability rules with strikes instead of silent suspensions; (d) *fast partial payout* is a strong cleaner-retention lever (Fiduxa waits 30 days); (e) staged onboarding with document/identity verification.

Sources: [how Zenjob works (companies)](https://www.zenjob.com/en/companies/how-does-zenjob-work), [employment](https://www.zenjob.com/en/jobs/employment), [first job](https://www.zenjob.com/en/jobs/first-job), [sign-up](https://www.zenjob.com/en/jobs/sign-up/), [requirements](https://www.zenjob.com/en/jobs/zenjob-requirements/), [booking platform](https://www.zenjob.com/en/booking-platform/), [Indeed Zenjob](https://de.indeed.com/cmp/Zenjob/reviews). ## 6. NVIDIA — AI infrastructure concepts (general knowledge; confidence Medium)

NVIDIA's public material (Triton inference server, RAPIDS data processing, predictive analytics case studies) is relevant to companies running their own models at scale. For Fiduxa's size (hundreds to low thousands of bookings/month) **none of it is appropriate**: a MongoDB aggregation and a nightly Node job answer every forecasting question Fiduxa has. The transferable idea is *batch the heavy work* (nightly reconciliation, pre-computed dashboards) — which Fiduxa already does for tiers and will do for finance snapshots. Recommendation: no GPU/ML infrastructure; revisit only if a model-based matching system is ever justified by volume.
